# Kenyan Payroll Statutory Review — Preliminary Source Register

**Status: NOT PROFESSIONALLY VERIFIED.** This is a technical source review, not legal, tax, or payroll advice. A qualified Kenyan payroll/tax professional must sign off the effective-dated rule pack before production use.

## Findings from official sources reviewed (18 November 2026)

| Item | Preliminary source finding | Implementation action |
|---|---|---|
| PAYE | KRA guidance says apply applicable tax bands and personal relief to resident employees. The KRA FAQ still labels its bands as Finance Act 2023; do not treat that page alone as proof the bands are current for 2026. | Verify bands, reliefs, residency, taxable benefits and effective dates against current Finance Act/Income Tax Act and KRA guidance. |
| Personal relief and other reliefs | KRA's 6 Oct 2025 notice says personal relief applies to resident employees and documented insurance, mortgage-interest, pension and post-retirement medical deductions/reliefs should be applied where eligible and within limits. | Add evidence collection, eligibility validation, limits and effective dates; never assume every employee qualifies. |
| SHIF | SHA employer notice states monthly SHIF contributions are due by the 9th and describes a 2% monthly late-remittance penalty. KRA's calculator describes SHIF as 2.75% of gross income with a low-income floor. | Confirm the current rate, floor, calculation base and remittance rules with SHA/KRA for the payroll period. Keep the rules effective-dated. |
| Affordable Housing Levy | KRA public notice states employee 1.5% and employer 1.5% of gross monthly salary, remitted by the 9th working day after month end. | Verify gross definition, relief, exemptions, filing/payment workflow and effective dates with current law. |
| NITA training levy | NITA's Levy Inspectorate page says KES 50 per employee per month, employer-paid (not a payroll deduction), due on the last working day of the month. | Verify current levy order, covered employees, due date, exemptions and employer registration status. |
| NSSF | NSSF has a Year 4 (2026) employer notice dated 18 Feb 2026. The official notice's rate table could not be extracted during this review. Older NSSF pages are not suitable for 2026 rates. | Obtain and verify the official 2026 Year 4 table; confirm Tier I/II limits, contribution rate, pensionable earnings and any opt-out/Tier II arrangements before enabling calculation. |

## Professional sign-off checklist

Reviewer should record name, credentials/role, review date, reviewed tax period, sources and versions, test scenarios, decisions, outstanding issues and approval. Review at least:

- PAYE bands, personal relief, insurance relief, mortgage-interest relief, pension and post-retirement medical relief, tax exemptions and taxable benefits.
- NSSF Year 4 2026 rates and contribution limits, employee/employer split, pensionable wage definitions and Tier II treatment.
- SHIF contribution formula, floor, gross-income definition, due date, penalty and exemptions.
- Affordable Housing Levy base, employee/employer split, relief treatment, due date and penalties.
- NITA employer levy, covered employee definition, due date and exemptions.
- HELB, court orders, pension and other voluntary deductions, priority, consent, caps and remittance rules.
- Leave pay, overtime, termination, severance, gratuity, partial month, arrears, backdated adjustments and non-cash benefits.
- PAYE return, NSSF SF24, SHIF and AHL return/reporting formats and filing/payment workflows.
