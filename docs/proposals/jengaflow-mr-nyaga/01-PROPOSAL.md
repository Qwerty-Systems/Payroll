## 1. Introduction

Qwerty Systems is pleased to submit this proposal for consideration by Mr Nyaga regarding the onboarding, configuration and implementation of JengaFlow.

JengaFlow is an existing workforce and business-operations platform, not a concept being proposed for development from scratch. It is already in use across organisations and projects including Dominion Outdoor, Nifunze, SakaTaka, View Academy and others. The proposed engagement is to understand the intended organisation's requirements, configure the relevant existing capabilities, and deliver agreed adaptations, integrations, migration, training and rollout.

The organization or business unit for which this proposal is intended, and Mr Nyaga's formal role in the engagement, should be recorded in the final statement of work.

## 2. Proposed business outcomes

The proposed solution can provide a centralized environment for:
- employee, contractor and workforce records;
- departments, teams, projects, sites and supervisor assignments;
- attendance, shifts, timesheets and approvals where required;
- payroll preparation, review and reporting within the validated scope;
- controlled document management and role-based access;
- operational dashboards, reports and exports;
- auditability of important actions and approvals.

The exact modules and workflows will be selected after a requirements discussion and demonstrated before they are committed to the final scope.

## 3. Scope of the proposed engagement

**Discovery:** Understand the organization, current systems, workforce size, sites, user roles, operating processes, reporting needs and implementation constraints.

**Workspace setup:** Provision the agreed workspace and configure the company structure, departments, locations, users, roles and permissions.

**Configuration and customization:** Enable the agreed existing JengaFlow modules and adapt approved forms, fields, workflows, notifications and reports. Any net-new development outside existing configurable capabilities will be separately described and estimated.

**Data and integrations:** Assess source data, prepare migration mapping, perform a sample import and reconcile results. Review requested integrations for provider/API availability, access, cost and feasibility. Only integrations listed in the signed scope are included.

**Testing and rollout:** Support agreed UAT, resolve in-scope issues, train nominated administrators and users, and complete the launch and handover checklist.

## 4. Key deliverables

1. Discovery summary and requirements baseline.
2. Configured JengaFlow workspace.
3. Organization structure, users, roles and permissions.
4. Configured in-scope modules and agreed workflow adaptations.
5. Migration and reconciliation report, if migration is included.
6. Agreed integrations, if included.
7. UAT scenarios, results and issue log.
8. User/admin guidance, training and handover.
9. Go-live checklist and support transition.

## 5. Delivery plan

The proposed phases are discovery and design; workspace configuration; workflow adaptation; data migration and integrations; UAT and training; and rollout/handover. A detailed schedule will follow once the organization, scope, data volumes and dependencies are confirmed.

## 6. Data protection and payroll controls

The implementation plan will document access permissions, workspace separation, secure handling of personal and payroll information, audit requirements, backup/recovery arrangements and support access. Data-processing responsibilities and confidentiality arrangements must be agreed before production information is transferred.

Payroll calculations, statutory settings and payment-related workflows require confirmation against the organization's approved policies and review by its designated payroll professionals. The presence of a configurable feature does not constitute legal or statutory compliance certification.

## 7. Responsibilities

**Qwerty Systems:** lead discovery, configure the platform, deliver agreed customization, support testing, provide documentation and training, and complete handover.

**Client organization:** appoint a sponsor and process owners; provide authorized sample data and process information; confirm business rules; nominate UAT users; and review and approve deliverables.

## 8. Commercials and next step

A separate quotation is provided as a draft schedule. Final fees will be determined by the confirmed user/workforce volumes, locations, selected modules, customization, migration, integrations, hosting and support requirements. No amount or delivery date is committed until the final scope and quotation are approved in writing.

We recommend a discovery meeting with Mr Nyaga and the relevant business and technical representatives. Qwerty Systems will then provide a final statement of work, delivery schedule and priced quotation.
