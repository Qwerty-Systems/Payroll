## The idea

JengaFlow is being developed to help organizations manage their people, worksites, attendance and payroll processes in one place.

## What the proposed solution covers

- **People:** worker profiles, employment details and documents.
- **Sites:** projects, sites, supervisors and worker assignments.
- **Time:** attendance, shifts, timesheets and approvals.
- **Payroll:** pay rules, allowances, deductions, payroll review and payslips.
- **Visibility:** workforce, attendance and labour-cost reports.
- **Control:** role-based access, organization separation and audit history.

## How we would deliver it

1. **Discover** — confirm Uniken's workforce, sites, payroll rules and current systems.
2. **Configure** — agree the organization structure, roles, workflows and data.
3. **Implement** — deliver the approved worker, site, attendance and payroll scope.
4. **Validate** — test with Uniken users and compare payroll results before launch.
5. **Launch** — train users, hand over documentation and begin agreed support.

## What we need from Uniken

A project contact, representatives from Operations, HR/Payroll, Finance and IT, sample data, current payroll rules, site structure and a list of required integrations.

## Important

JengaFlow is an active product under development. This proposal describes the target solution and work to be completed; it does not claim that every feature is already production-ready. Payroll and tenant-access workflows must pass agreed tests before live use.

**Next step:** a discovery workshop, followed by a confirmed scope, delivery plan and priced quotation.
