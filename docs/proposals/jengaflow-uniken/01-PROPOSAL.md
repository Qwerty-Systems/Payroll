## 1. Introduction

Qwerty Systems is pleased to present this proposal for the onboarding, configuration and implementation of JengaFlow for Uniken.

JengaFlow is an existing workforce and business-operations platform, not a concept being proposed for development from scratch. It is already in use across organisations and projects including Dominion Outdoor, Nifunze, SakaTaka, View Academy and others. The proposed engagement is to assess Uniken's operating requirements, configure the relevant existing platform capabilities, and deliver the agreed adaptations, integrations, migration, training and rollout.

The precise modules and workflows to be enabled for Uniken will be confirmed during discovery and demonstrated before they are accepted into the final scope.

## 2. Proposed outcomes

The engagement aims to give Uniken a centralized and controlled platform for:
- maintaining employee, contractor and workforce records;
- organizing departments, teams, projects, sites and supervisors;
- managing attendance, shifts, timesheets and approvals where required;
- supporting payroll preparation, review and reporting within the validated scope;
- managing documents and operational records with role-based access;
- improving visibility through dashboards, reports and exports;
- maintaining an auditable record of important actions and approvals.

## 3. Proposed engagement

**A. Discovery and requirements confirmation**
- Workshops with Uniken's management, HR, finance, operations and IT representatives.
- Review of existing processes, forms, spreadsheets and systems.
- Confirmation of workforce volumes, locations, user roles and approval flows.
- Identification of required modules, reports, integrations and migration needs.
- Agreed implementation plan, acceptance criteria and final statement of work.

**B. Workspace setup and configuration**
- Create and configure the Uniken workspace.
- Configure company structure, departments, locations and operational units.
- Set up user roles, permissions and approval responsibilities.
- Configure selected existing modules and business settings.

**C. Workflow customization**
- Adapt agreed forms, fields, workflows, notifications and reports.
- Configure attendance, timesheet and payroll-related processes as confirmed.
- Record any development that falls outside existing configurable capabilities as a separately estimated change.

**D. Data migration and integrations**
- Assess source data and agree migration mapping.
- Run a sample import and reconcile results before production migration.
- Assess requested third-party integrations for API availability, access, cost and feasibility.
- Deliver only integrations expressly included in the approved scope.

**E. Testing, training and rollout**
- Prepare agreed user acceptance test scenarios.
- Support Uniken's nominated users through UAT and issue resolution.
- Provide administrator and end-user training.
- Complete the agreed launch checklist and operational handover.

## 4. Deliverables

1. Discovery summary and approved requirements baseline.
2. Configured Uniken JengaFlow workspace.
3. Agreed organization, user roles and access permissions.
4. Configured in-scope modules and customized workflows.
5. Approved data migration and reconciliation outputs, if migration is included.
6. Agreed integrations, if included in the signed scope.
7. UAT scenarios, results and issue log.
8. User/admin guides, training and handover documentation.
9. Go-live checklist and post-launch support transition.

## 5. Delivery approach and timing

Delivery will be phased: discovery and design; workspace configuration; workflow adaptation; migration/integrations; UAT and training; then rollout and handover. A dated project schedule will be issued after the discovery scope, data volumes, integrations and client dependencies are confirmed. No unverified delivery date is committed in this discussion draft.

## 6. Security, privacy and operational controls

The final design will document role-based access, tenant/workspace separation, secure handling of personal and payroll data, audit requirements, backup and recovery arrangements, support access and data retention. Uniken will confirm its policies and approve the applicable data-processing and confidentiality arrangements before production data is transferred.

Payroll calculations, statutory settings and any payment-related workflow must be validated against Uniken's approved policies and reviewed by its designated payroll professionals. A configurable feature is not, by itself, a legal or statutory compliance certification.

## 7. Responsibilities

**Qwerty Systems:** discovery, solution configuration, agreed customization, technical delivery, testing support, documentation, training and handover.

**Uniken:** appoint a project sponsor and process owners; provide authorized sample data and existing process documentation; confirm business rules; review deliverables; nominate UAT participants; and provide timely decisions and approvals.

The separate quotation is a commercial schedule template pending confirmation of the final scope. One-time implementation, migration, integrations, hosting, maintenance, support and optional services will be itemized separately. No amount is represented as approved until a final priced quotation is issued by Qwerty Systems.
